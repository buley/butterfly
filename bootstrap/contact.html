Frequently Asked Questions

When is the best time to plant my butterfly box?

Spring and early fall are ideal planting times in our region. This gives plants time to establish before extreme temperatures arrive. We recommend planting on a cool, overcast day to minimize transplant shock.

How much maintenance do butterfly boxes require?

Our butterfly boxes are designed to be low-maintenance. Regular watering during the first growing season is essential, but once established, most native plants require minimal care. Your box will come with specific care instructions.

Can I order butterfly boxes for a school or community garden?

Absolutely! We offer special pricing for educational institutions and community projects. Please contact us directly to discuss your needs and we can create a custom solution.

How can I volunteer with Sackett Ranch?

We welcome volunteers who are passionate about monarch conservation! We have opportunities in plant propagation, garden maintenance, educational outreach, and special events. Fill out our contact form to express interest.

Do you ship butterfly boxes outside of the local area?

We primarily serve the Northern California region to ensure plants arrive in excellent condition. For locations beyond our standard delivery area, please contact us to discuss options.

What if I spot monarchs in my butterfly box?

Congratulations! This means your habitat is working. We encourage you to log your monarch sightings with the Monarch Watch citizen science program. Feel free to share photos with us on social media too!
